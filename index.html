Privacy Policy

App: SideNote: Floating Sticky Notes (com.sidedeck.notes)

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview & Core Privacy Promise

Welcome to SideNote. We believe your thoughts, quick notes, and personal checklists are private. SideNote is designed from the ground up as a local-first, privacy-focused productivity tool.

Summary: SideNote does not collect, track, upload, or sell your personal information. Your notes, checklist items, and files remain strictly stored on your own local device.

2. Information Collection & Storage

  • User Notes & Checklists: All notes, task lists, formatting choices, and color themes are stored exclusively on your device's local internal storage.
  • File Attachments: Any images or documents attached to notes are stored in your device's private app sandbox.
  • No Cloud Accounts: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address to use SideNote.
  • No Analytic Trackers: We do not integrate third-party tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

SideNote requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core multitasking functionality:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables the floating sticky notes and the quick edge-drawer overlay so you can jot down thoughts while multitasking over other applications.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used solely to keep the edge swipe handle responsive and ready to summon when you swipe from any screen.
  • Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when you tap the voice dictation shortcut to convert speech to text on your device. Audio is processed directly through Android's built-in speech recognition and is never recorded or sent to private servers.
  • Local Network & Wi-Fi (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, INTERNET): Used exclusively for direct local peer-to-peer Wi-Fi synchronization with SideNote for Windows PC.

4. Local Wi-Fi PC Sync (Zero Cloud)

When you choose to pair SideNote with your Windows computer:

  • Data synchronization happens exclusively over your local home/office Wi-Fi network.
  • Communication occurs directly between your phone and your PC over an authenticated, encrypted peer-to-peer connection.
  • Your notes and attachments never transit through or get stored on any external cloud server or remote database.

5. In-App Purchases & Third Parties

If you purchase SideNote Pro, billing transactions are securely handled by Google Play In-App Billing.

  • We do not collect, process, or store credit card or payment details.
  • Transactions are governed by the Google Play Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can edit or permanently delete any note, checklist, or attachment at any time directly within the application.
  • Uninstalling the SideNote application automatically and permanently erases all locally stored notes and app data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

SideNote does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
